Parent / teacher guide

A simple weekly rhythm

  1. Day 1 — Learn: Open the weekly lesson, watch the supporting video, work examples, and introduce the garage scenario.
  2. Day 2 — Practice: Open or print the student worksheet and complete the first half.
  3. Day 3 — Apply: Do the hands-on garage challenge or measurement activity.
  4. Day 4 — Finish & Check: Complete the worksheet and use the separate answer key to review mistakes.

Tip: Real measurements make the lessons better. Use a tape measure, stopwatch, sockets, scrap wood/metal, tire gauges, catalogs, receipts, or race data whenever safe and practical.
